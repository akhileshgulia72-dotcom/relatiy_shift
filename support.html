Common questions

How do hints and skips work? Tap Hint and finish the rewarded ad to reveal guidance. Skip becomes available after a hint reward has been earned; skipping requires its own rewarded ad. If an ad is unavailable, you can continue playing.

How do I restart a puzzle? Use the restart control on the level screen to reset that level's attempt.

Where is my progress? Progress and settings are saved on your device. Removing the app or clearing its data may remove local progress.

How do I change the music? Open Settings in the game and use the music switch.
